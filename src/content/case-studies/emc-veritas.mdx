## Problem

Certificates and leadership recognition are only useful when recipients and third parties can trust them. The Event Management Club needed a practical way to issue credentials while giving anyone a clear path to verify them.

## Requirement

The platform needed to support certificate issuance, leadership recognition, and public verification without making the process difficult for the organization or the person checking a credential.

## Approach

I focused the product around a simple trust loop: create a durable record, attach a verification path, and make the result understandable in a browser. That kept the problem grounded in operational use rather than treating QR codes as a decorative feature.

## Build

EMC Veritas combines a React interface, a FastAPI backend, a Supabase-backed data layer, and QR-driven verification. The system is designed to make issued records easy to manage while providing a direct public verification experience.

## Technical decisions and challenges

Verification systems need to be clear at the moment of use. The data model and verification route were designed to keep the credential lookup path focused and to separate issuance workflows from the public verification surface. Production hardening centered on making that public path dependable without exposing issuance administration.

## Result

EMC Veritas is live. It provides a concrete foundation for issuing and checking organization credentials through a public verification experience.
